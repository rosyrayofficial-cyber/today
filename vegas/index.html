Vegas hunts for market leaders breaking out of a base, the pattern behind most big 3-month runs. It ranks every candidate in eight steps:

  1. Market first. Checks SPY against its 10, 30 and 40-week averages. In a correction most breakouts fail, so confidence is cut and capped.
  2. Trend template. Eight checks borrowed from Minervini: price above the 30 and 40-week MAs, MAs stacked and rising, at least 30% above the 52-week low, and within 25% of the high.
  3. Relative strength. 3, 6, 9 and 12-month performance against SPY, weighted toward the latest quarter, plus whether the RS line is at a new high.
  4. The setup. Finds the base and its pivot (the buy point), the base depth and tightness, and whether the breakout came on heavy volume. It never chases more than 5% past the pivot.
  5. Fuel. Weekly volatility (a slow stock can't do +40%), accumulation (volume heavier on up weeks), and earnings and sales growth, margins and analyst upside.
  6. House odds. Backtests the stock's own history: every time it looked like this, how often did it hit +40% before −10% within 13 weeks?
  7. AI read. DeepSeek builds a 90-day thesis: catalysts and their timing, bull, base and bear cases with odds, whether the hype is real, and what would invalidate the trade. It also sees how past picks turned out.
  8. The play. Buy zone at the pivot. Stop 7–10% below. Sell a third at +20% and move the stop to break-even. Sell another third at +40%, and the rest at +50% or on a weekly close below the 10-week MA. Exit by week 6 if it isn't up 10%. Hard horizon of 13 weeks.

Honest math: +40–50% in 3 months is a tail outcome. Most picks will stop out or stall, and that's expected. The system works because each loser costs about 1R while one winner pays 4–5R. Your track record keeps score, and DeepSeek sees it on every spin.
